## Design Principles

- Karpathy-style working loop for this project: write down the context, keep the system simple, improve the data/assets, ship small visible changes, evaluate with screenshots, and remove anything that creates confusion.
- The page should feel like a modern neighborhood store website, not a marketing landing page and not a heavy animated site.
- Prioritize customer actions: call, directions, hours, product request, and what can be picked up quickly.
- Make each visual panel serve a distinct customer job. Avoid duplicated tequila/premium bottle content.
- Use careful language: "ask about", "request", "pairing idea", "shown from reels", "available today" where needed.

## Current Direction

- The site should use a clean, mobile-friendly single-page structure.
- The homepage should quickly answer:
  - What is this store?
  - Where is it?
  - Is it open?
  - What can I request?
  - What can I grab in one stop?
- Keep local preview and browser verification before asking to push.

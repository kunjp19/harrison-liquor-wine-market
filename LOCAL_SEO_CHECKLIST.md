# Harrison Local SEO Checklist

Use this checklist when updating the website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, DoorDash, Nextdoor, or other public citations.

## Verified Store Facts

- Name: Harrison Liquor & Wine Market
- Address: 735 Harrison Blvd, Lincoln Park, MI 48146
- Phone: (313) 389-3759
- Hours: Sunday-Thursday 10:00 AM-10:00 PM; Friday-Saturday 10:00 AM-11:00 PM
- Website: https://harrisonliquorwinemarket.vercel.app/

## Ranking Priorities

1. Add the website URL to the correct Google Business Profile.
2. Keep name, address, phone, and hours identical on Google, Facebook, Instagram, DoorDash, Nextdoor, and the website.
3. Post weekly Google Business Profile updates for current offers and new arrivals.
4. Ask satisfied adult customers to leave Google reviews using the official review link.
5. Add only approved review excerpts to the website. Do not add fake star ratings or unsupported review schema.
6. Confirm the exact current Facebook page before adding it to website structured data.
7. Use real product/store images and avoid repeated or inaccurate product imagery.

## Review Integration Rules

- Google reviews help most when they are on Google Business Profile.
- The website may show short approved excerpts later, but should link customers back to the Google listing.
- Do not mark up self-selected local business reviews with review-rich-result schema.
- Keep age-restricted product language factual and not youth-focused.

## Weekly Content Refresh

- Update `data/featured-offers.json` when posted offers change.
- Update offer pages only for verified product names, sizes, and prices.
- Update `sitemap.xml` if a new public page is added.
- Rebuild and validate before deploying.
